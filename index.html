I work on making single-cell biology searchable and comparable at scale.

My research sits at the intersection of foundation models and translational biology — building systems that turn massive single-cell datasets into tools scientists actually use, from cell-level search to patient-level disease modeling.

SCimilarity — a search engine for cells

Think of it as reverse image search for cells: hand the model a cell, and it returns every similar cell we've ever seen — along with the tissue, disease, and context that came with them.
